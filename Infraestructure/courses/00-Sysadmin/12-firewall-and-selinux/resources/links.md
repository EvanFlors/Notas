# sys-12 — Recursos

## nftables

- **nftables wiki (oficial, Netfilter Project)** — https://wiki.nftables.org/
  La referencia canónica. Empieza por "Quick reference-nftables in 10 minutes" y "Simple ruleset for a server".
- **`man 8 nft`** — sintaxis completa en local.
- **Netfilter hooks** — https://wiki.nftables.org/wiki-nftables/index.php/Netfilter_hooks

## firewalld

- **firewalld docs** — https://firewalld.org/documentation/
- **`man firewalld.richlanguage`** — rich rules.
- **RHEL 9 "Configuring firewalls and packet filters"** — https://access.redhat.com/documentation/en-us/red_hat_enterprise_linux/9/html/configuring_firewalls_and_packet_filters/

## ufw

- **Ubuntu Server Guide — Firewall** — https://ubuntu.com/server/docs/security-firewall
- **`man ufw`**

## SELinux

- **SELinux Project Notebook (GitHub)** — https://github.com/SELinuxProject/selinux-notebook
  La referencia conceptual más clara y gratuita que existe.
- **Red Hat Enterprise Linux 9 — "Using SELinux"** — https://access.redhat.com/documentation/en-us/red_hat_enterprise_linux/9/html/using_selinux/
- **`man 8 semanage`**, **`man 8 restorecon`**, **`man 1 audit2allow`**.
- **"Your visual how-to guide for SELinux policy enforcement"** (poster de Dan Walsh) — https://opensource.com/business/13/11/selinux-policy-guide
